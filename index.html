Spatial Analysis of Lakaki Lake

As part of our cpastone project, my group-mates and I conducted a comprehensive spatial assessment and buffer modeling examining surrounding urban encroachment, water body dynamics, and environmental parameters.

Spatio-Temporal Analysis

Multi-temporal shoreline and invasive species boundary tracking from 2016 to 2026.

  • Evaluates historical lake boundary fluctuations and long-term morphological shifts.
  • Monitors the changing spatial extent of invasive aquatic species over a ten-year span.

Anthropogenic Pressure Zones

Buffer zone analysis assessing surrounding urban activity and infrastructure impacts.

  • Maps buffer distances around point sources of urban activity near the lake perimeter.
  • Identifies high-stress areas susceptible to human encroachment and land-use pressure.

NDBI Analysis

Normalized Difference Built-Up Index tracking urban expansion around the water body.

  • Highlights the intensification of built-up surfaces in the immediate surrounding neighborhoods.
  • Provides comparative index values across 2016, 2021, and 2026 to measure urban density growth.

NDRE Analysis

Normalized Difference Red Edge analysis evaluating vegetation health and chlorophyll content.

  • Utilizes red-edge bands to assess canopy health and stress levels in lakeside vegetation.
  • Tracks multi-temporal variations in vegetative vigor and density near the water margin.

NDWI Analysis

Normalized Difference Water Index mapping surface water dynamics and moisture changes.

  • Delineates open water bodies and highlights surface moisture retention trends over time.
  • Measures seasonal and multi-year fluctuations in water spread versus surrounding dry land.

NDVI Analysis

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index measuring greenness and biomass distribution.

  • Quantifies density of green vegetation and spatial distribution of parkland around the lake.
  • Compares vegetation index shifts to evaluate ecological changes across the study periods.
